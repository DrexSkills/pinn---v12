How it works

Everything you need to know about using Pinn.

Shortcut

One combo summons Pinn from anywhere — even over fullscreen apps.

⌘ Shift P
Show / Hide
Toggle Pinn from any app. Works over fullscreen, video calls, anything.
Ctrl Shift P
Windows
Same behaviour on Windows. Ctrl replaces Cmd automatically.
Esc
Dismiss
Close Pinn instantly without touching the mouse.

Chat

A focused interface powered by a fast AI model. No clutter.

Enter
Send
Send your message. The AI streams the reply as it's generated.
Shift Enter
New line
Add a line break without sending — for multi-line questions or code.
Memory
Persistent context
Pinn remembers context across sessions automatically.

Screenshot

Capture any part of your screen and send it straight to the AI.

macOS
Capture
Pinn hides itself. Use ⌘ Shift 4 to select an area — Pinn picks it up automatically.
Windows
Capture
Pinn takes a silent snapshot and shows an overlay. Drag to select your area.
Auto attach
Preview
A thumbnail appears above the input. Type your question and hit Enter.

Sessions

Every conversation is saved automatically. Pick up where you left off.

New chat
Start fresh
Opens a blank conversation. Previous session is saved automatically.
History
Browse past chats
Click the sessions icon in the header. Tap any entry to resume.
Storage
Where they live
Saved as JSON files in ~/.pinn-sessions/ — back up or delete them directly.

Settings

Personalise Pinn from the gear icon in the header.

Theme
5 colour themes
White, Dark, Black, Blue, or Pink. Switches instantly.
Font size
Small · Medium · Large
Three sizes for any display scaling.
Hotkey
Custom shortcut
Click the field, press your combo, save. Registers system-wide immediately.

Privacy

Pinn leaves no trace. What you type stays on your machine.

OS invisible
No dock, no switcher
No Dock, no Mission Control, no Cmd+Tab. Windows: no taskbar, no Alt+Tab.
Screen share
Invisible to recording
Content protection at OS level — invisible to Zoom, Teams, Meet, OBS.
Local only
Nothing leaves your device
No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Everything stored in plain JSON on your machine.

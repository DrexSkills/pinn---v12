Documentation

How every feature works

Shortcut

One combo summons Pinn from anywhere — even over fullscreen apps.

⌘ Shift P
Show / Hide
Toggle Pinn from any app. Works over fullscreen, video calls, anything.
Ctrl Shift P
Windows shortcut
Same behaviour on Windows. Ctrl replaces Cmd automatically.
Esc
Dismiss
Close Pinn immediately without touching the mouse.

Chat

A focused chat interface powered by a fast AI model. No clutter.

Enter
Send message
Send your message. The AI streams the reply as it's generated.
Shift Enter
New line
Add a line break without sending — useful for multi-line questions.
Memory
Persistent context
Pinn retains context across sessions automatically.

Screenshot

Capture any part of your screen and send it straight to the AI.

Camera icon
macOS
Pinn hides itself. Use ⌘ Shift 4 to select an area. Pinn picks it up automatically.
Camera icon
Windows
Pinn takes a silent snapshot and shows a dark overlay. Drag to select your area.
Auto attach
Thumbnail preview
A thumbnail appears above the input. Type your question and hit Enter.

Sessions

Every conversation is saved automatically. Pick up where you left off.

New chat
Start fresh
Opens a blank conversation. Previous session is saved automatically.
History panel
Browse past chats
Click the sessions icon in the header. Tap any entry to resume.
Storage
Where sessions live
Saved as JSON files in ~/.pinn-sessions/. You can back up or delete them directly.

Settings

Personalise Pinn from the gear icon in the header.

Theme
5 colour themes
White, Dark, Black, Blue, or Pink. Switches instantly.
Font size
Small · Medium · Large
Three sizes for any display scaling.
Hotkey
Custom shortcut
Click the hotkey field, press your combo, save. Registers system-wide immediately.

Privacy

Pinn leaves no trace. What you type stays on your machine.

OS invisible
No dock, no switcher
No Dock icon, no Mission Control, no Cmd+Tab. Windows: no taskbar, no Alt+Tab.
Screen share
Invisible to recording
Content protection enabled at OS level — invisible to Zoom, Teams, Meet, OBS.
Local only
Nothing leaves your device
Chat history and settings stored in plain JSON on your machine. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.
